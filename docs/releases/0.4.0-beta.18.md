> **Never released.** Its changes are in [0.4.0-beta.19](0.4.0-beta.19.md).

Shared Tasks 0.4.0-beta.18: the eighteenth beta with shared sections, and
the candidate for 0.4.0.

A pre-release for testers through BRAT: the community directory and
Obsidian's own updates keep offering 0.3.x.

## New since 0.4.0-beta.16

**Updating from Shared Tasks 0.3.4 now always starts syncing.** When
Shared Tasks 0.3.4 was updated to a 0.4 beta in the same Obsidian window
(as Obsidian's own plugin update and BRAT do), the new version could find
the old one's data still in use and fail to start. Nothing synced, your
shared tasks stopped updating, every section showed "Loading shared
section", and nothing said why until Obsidian was restarted. It now waits
for the old version to finish and tries again for a few seconds. If the
vault really is open in another Obsidian window, it says so once and
starts by itself when that window closes.

**A start that fails says so.** If Shared Tasks cannot start for any other
reason, one notice now says "Shared Tasks could not start: …" and what to
do (restart Obsidian; your notes are not affected), instead of staying
silent.

**A note open only in Reading view now shows a collaborator's changes.**
If a note with a shared section was open only in Reading view (not in
Source or Live Preview), a collaborator's changes never appeared in it,
although the section said it was up to date, and switching back to
editing then lost them from your note. They now show in Reading view and
stay when you switch back, and the collaborator's change is never undone.

**A section after a collaborator was removed loads for newcomers.** When
someone's access to a section was removed, a collaborator invited
afterwards could stay at "no shared section on this device yet" for good,
in a section where nobody edited since. The section now loads for them
within seconds.

**"1 local update waiting" no longer stays after everything was sent.**
After going offline or a server restart, a section could keep saying an
update was waiting although it had been sent and accepted. It now says the
section is up to date.

**Reconnecting within 15 seconds.** After the sync server or the network
was unreachable for a few minutes, Shared Tasks could take up to a minute
to reconnect after it came back. It now tries again at most every 15
seconds, and at once when you switch back to Obsidian's window.

**Moving a task by cut and paste no longer deletes it for collaborators
when Obsidian is busy.** If you cut a task and pasted it elsewhere in the
section while Shared Tasks was still writing a change into the note, the
task could be deleted for your collaborators while your note still showed
it, and the section said it needed attention. The paste now always moves
the task.

**No false "needs attention" after a collaborator deletes a task.** When a
collaborator deleted a task, your section could say it differs from the
shared version, and "Review conflicts" offered to restore a task your note
no longer showed, until you next typed in that note. The task is now
removed and the section is up to date.

**Review conflicts no longer offers a place it cannot apply.** When a task
was moved by one collaborator while another deleted the task it was moved
under, "Review conflicts" offered to put it under the deleted task;
choosing that said it could not be applied, and the question stayed. Only
places that can be applied are offered now, and the top of the section
when no other is left.

**A copy that differs says so when the shared version hides a task.** A
task your note showed under another task that the shared version no
longer shows could stay in your note while your collaborators did not
have it, and the section said it was up to date. It now says your note
differs from the shared version.

**No "Server confirmation unavailable" for changes that were saved.**
After a long pause in Obsidian, a section could say "Server confirmation
unavailable" for changes the server had saved. It now says they are
confirmed.

**Memory stays flat as a section's history grows.** A section's memory
grew with every change (in long sessions, hundreds of megabytes), and
editing slowed down with it. It now stays the same however long the
section's history is.

**Tested before release: installing and updating.** On Obsidian 1.13.4 and
1.14.4, all checked against a collaborator:
- a fresh install;
- an update from 0.3.4 with shared tasks and an edit made offline: your
  data is upgraded, the edit is sent, the tasks keep syncing both ways,
  nothing lost or doubled;
- an update from 0.4.0-beta.15 with a shared section;
- turning the plugin off and on, and uninstalling and installing it again:
  your identity and sections are kept.

**Tested before release: themes, views and copying.** On Obsidian 1.13.4
and 1.14.4, with a collaborator editing: light and dark theme in Source,
Live Preview and Reading view; a note in Reading view next to another in
Live Preview; the same note in two panes; moving between panes; the Tasks
plugin on and off; and tasks copied into a note outside any section.

The details are in the [support matrix](../devel/testing/support-matrix-0.2.md).

**Requires Obsidian 1.13.4 or later.**

## What it is

The features of 0.4.0, described in [0.4.0.md](0.4.0.md): share a whole
section of a note ("Share section…"), with its tasks, paragraphs and lists,
end-to-end encrypted, each section its own collaboration. Shared sections
are on in this beta: there is nothing to turn on.

## Before you install

> **No way back to 0.3.x on this device.** The first start of 0.4 upgrades
> Shared Tasks' sync data on the device and encrypts it at rest. A 0.3.x
> plugin can no longer open it ("Shared Tasks could not start:
> VersionError"); install 0.4.x again and the data is intact. Your notes are
> not affected.

- Try it in a test vault first, or keep a copy of your vault.
- A collaborator still on 0.3.x cannot join a shared section; their shared
  tasks keep working with yours.
- Collaborators on a shared section should all update to this beta. A
  member on a beta before 0.4.0-beta.14 can keep undoing the others' edits
  while their note is open, and a member on a beta before 0.4.0-beta.4
  keeps seeing "Needs your attention" after someone else chose **Keep it
  deleted**.

## Install with BRAT

1. Install BRAT from the community plugins.
2. Run "BRAT: Add a beta plugin for testing" with `openlfcp/obsidian`. In
   the version field choose **Latest version**: a specific version stays
   installed, and BRAT will not update it.
3. Enable Shared Tasks. Settings → Community plugins lists it as
   version 0.4.0-beta.18. If it lists an earlier version, see below.

If an earlier 0.4.0 beta is installed, BRAT updates it ("BRAT: Check for
updates to all beta plugins"); your identity, collaborations and settings
are kept.

**Stuck on an older beta?** In BRAT's settings, find `openlfcp/obsidian` in
the beta plugin list. If it shows a version, it is pinned there: edit it and
choose **Latest version** (or remove it and add it again with Latest
version), then run "BRAT: Check for updates to all beta plugins". BRAT's
setting "Select latest plugin version by default" saves choosing.

To turn shared sections off, quit Obsidian, add `"sectionsDisabled": true`
to `.obsidian/plugins/shared-tasks/data.json`, and start it again.

## What to report

"Export diagnostics…" prepares a report without note text, names, paths,
keys or links, and shows it to you first. Attach it to an issue at
https://github.com/openlfcp/obsidian/issues with what you did and what you
saw.

## Known limits

The limits of [0.4.0.md](0.4.0.md#limits) apply: about 200 tasks per
section is the qualified size, sections are read-only on mobile, and there
are no headings inside a section.

- **Rejoining a section you were removed from** isn't supported yet. Your
  local copy stays on your device.
- **Project server quotas.** The project server (sync.openlfcp.org)
  currently lets one identity host up to 200 collaborations (each shared
  section is one), up to 50 new ones a day from one network, 256 MiB of
  stored data in all and 128 MiB per collaboration. Past a limit, the
  action is refused with "quota exceeded"; nothing already shared is lost.
  These values may change, and the server's operator can set other limits
  for an identity. A server you run yourself sets its own.

## Under the hood

- The runtime takes the vault's writer lock before it opens its database:
  0.3.4 and later release the lock only after closing their database, so
  the upgrade of the database to version 2 is no longer blocked by the old
  connection. A start blocked by a connection still closing is tried again
  every 500 ms for up to 10 s; a start that finds the lock held by another
  window is tried again every 5 s in the background, until the plugin is
  unloaded. A start whose database does not open releases the lock.
- Any start failure is said once ("Shared Tasks could not start: …").
- New native spec `upgrade-install`: a fresh install, updates from 0.3.4
  and 0.4.0-beta.15 (the server down at the update), disable and enable,
  uninstall and reinstall. Its results are the "Install and upgrade"
  section of the support matrix.
- A note shown only in Reading view is synced through its file: Obsidian
  keeps a note's editor behind its Reading view and never saves what is
  written there. Back to editing, the editor takes the file as written
  before the note is synced again, and a sync under way when the note
  went into Reading view is dropped (the editor's view routing follows
  Obsidian's layout-change).
- New native spec `section-matrix`: light and dark theme × Source, Live
  Preview and Reading view, per-note routing, split view, focus, and the
  clipboard. Its results are the desktop sections of the support matrix.
- The SDK side, an IndexedDB open that waits instead of failing when
  another connection blocks it, is LFCP-02-120, after 0.2.
- A member who may publish Snapshots publishes one as soon as the current
  Data Epoch (past 0) has none on this device, checked every 10 s: a
  member who joins after a removal receives only the new epoch's key and
  loads the history from that Snapshot. An invitation waits until the
  server has it.
- The SDK status the host keeps (SdkStatusTracker) checks a batch in
  storage after each receipt release and each report that is not final: a
  batch the SDK no longer holds leaves the pending ones.
- The reconnect policy waits 1 s, doubling, at most 15 s, each wait
  within ±20 %; focusing the window connects at once.
- A pass's result describes the note as written: a node its remote patch
  removes is not reported shown again. A pass that wrote a collaborator's
  change and still reports the note as differing is followed by exactly
  one more pass, never a loop.
- A paste made while a pass is still writing is not dropped: the source
  coordinator counts each route's changes, and a pass's write keeps a
  source that changed since the pass started, so the next pass sees the
  paste with its hints (soak finding #9).
- Review conflicts offers no placement under a deleted node.
- A node active in the model but hidden, still shown in the note, counts
  as a divergence.
- `@openlfcp/*` 0.2.0-beta.9: an acknowledgement that arrives after a
  long pause is recorded as the server's confirmation; and a section's replica no longer keeps an old
  copy of the document per unchanged task (Automerge memory flat as the
  history grows: 10 MiB at 2,000 changes with 170 tasks, against 1.6 GiB).
- New native spec `section-remove-invite`: remove someone, invite someone
  new, the newcomer loads (also with the plugin reloaded in between).
- Unchanged: spec `mvp-0.2-baseline.12`, the reference server at
  `138030a`, 49 fixtures.
