# Shared Tasks 0.7.0

Shared Tasks 0.7.0 adds linked devices: one person, several devices. Pair
your laptop, desktop and phone, and your collaborators see one person,
not three installations. For catalog users updating from 0.6.1 or 0.6.0.

**Requires Obsidian 1.13.4 or later**, as 0.6.x. **Linked devices need a
sync server with device support**: the default server, sync.openlfcp.org,
has it.

The Markdown format of your notes is unchanged. The sync protocol gains
device records; a collaborator on an older version is affected only once
you add your first device (see "Before you add your first device").

## What changed since 0.6.1

- **Add a device.** "Add my other device…" on the device you already use
  makes a link. On the new device (a fresh vault with Shared Tasks), run
  "Pair this device with a link…" and paste it. Both screens show a
  fingerprint: compare them, then approve on your first device with an
  access level: **Full** (a computer), **Edit** (a phone), **Read**, or
  **Service** (a bridge, for chosen sections only). Your shared sections
  open on the new device.
- **Your devices.** "Your devices…" lists them with their access, and
  removes one. Before it removes a device, it says which devices stop with
  it. A removed device is told so; to use that vault again, pair a new,
  empty vault instead.
- **People, not installations.** Where someone uses several devices, the
  access list shows them once, with their devices inside. Edits, local
  names and assignments count for the person, with the device as detail.
  A local name you give someone is theirs on all their devices.
- **Any Full device works for you.** A device with Full access shares
  sections, accepts invitations and creates sections as you: they are
  yours on all your devices, and sections you create later reach your
  other devices.
- **Paced device steps.** Each device step talks to the sync server, which
  allows about 20 new connections a minute from one network. Shared Tasks
  paces the steps, and the device dialogs ask less often the longer they
  wait (after 4, 8 and 15 seconds, then every 30 seconds); **Check now**
  asks at once.
- A guide: [Your devices](../guides/devices.md).

## Before you add your first device

Adding your first device changes each section you share so that your
devices can open it. A collaborator on **Shared Tasks 0.6.0 or earlier**
stops syncing those sections (0.6.0 shows them as offline); **0.6.1** says
"This section now uses a person's linked devices… Update the plugin".
After they update to 0.7.0, the sections sync again, with nothing lost on
their side. Tell your collaborators before you add your first device.

Nothing changes for anyone until someone adds a device: 0.7.0 alone syncs
with 0.6.x as before.

## Install and upgrade

From Settings → Community plugins → Updates. Nothing to migrate: identity,
keys, shared sections, queued edits, settings and hotkeys are kept. Your
existing installation becomes your first device when you add another one.

By hand: put `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from the release
page into `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/shared-tasks/` and compare them with
these checksums.

## Rolling back to 0.6.1

Before you add a device: install 0.6.1 over 0.7.0 (BRAT: choose version
0.6.1 for `openlfcp/obsidian`, or put 0.6.1's three files into the plugin
folder); nothing else changes.

After you added a device, 0.6.1 cannot follow the sections your devices
use: they stop with "This section now uses a person's linked devices…
Update the plugin", your copy stays readable, and edits wait until you
install 0.7.0 again. Your other devices keep working on 0.7.0.

## Known limits

As in [0.6.0](0.6.0.md#known-limits), plus:

- On a phone, shared sections stay read-only, whatever the device's access.
- A second device with Full access cannot see the requests of a link shown
  by another device: approve on the device that shows the link.
- `lfcp-node pair` (pairing a headless bridge) does not start on Windows;
  pair a bridge from Linux or macOS. No bridge that reads or writes notes
  ships yet.
- Access is chosen once: to change a device's access, remove the device,
  then pair a new, empty vault on it with the access you want.
