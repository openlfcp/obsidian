# Shared Tasks 0.6.0

Shared Tasks 0.6.0 redraws the Shared Tasks pane: a row of icons at the top,
one card for the place the cursor is on, one highlighted button for the next
step, and everything else in a menu or in the section's details. For catalog
users updating from 0.5.2.

**Requires Obsidian 1.13.4 or later**, as 0.5.2.

The Markdown format of your notes and the sync protocol are unchanged.

## What changed since 0.5.2

- **A row of icons along the top of the pane.** Under the title
  **Shared Tasks**: **"Share section…"**, **"Insert into a note…"**,
  **"Join with a link…"** and, at the right end, **"More actions"** ("…").
  The row is always the same; a dimmed icon is blocked, and its tooltip says
  why. Point at an icon to see its name.
- **One card for the place the cursor is on.** The section's name, its
  status in a word with an icon (for example **Synced**, **Not syncing**,
  **Offline**, **Read-only**), one line of detail, and one highlighted
  button for the next step, with **"Open details"** beside it. A problem
  turns the card red, and its button is the fix. When there is nothing to
  do, there is no highlighted button.
- **Details hold the fine print.** Times, the server, caveats and the full
  list of what is waiting moved from the pane's surface to **"Open
  details"**. A server's refusal is said in words on the card; its code is
  in the details.
- **Rare and destructive actions are in "More actions".** For example
  "Copy readable text", "Copy shared section", "Sync details", and, each
  with its usual confirmation, "Remove access…", "Discard the local copy…"
  and "Detach this section".
- **One problem, one line, one button.** A problem the card already states
  is not listed again under it. Other problems are listed under the card,
  each with its place (for example "Other.md, line 31") and its own button,
  including another copy of the section that holds edits, or a damaged
  marker of another section in the same note. The card's button is always
  for the section under the cursor.
- **The section list shows one line per section**, for example
  "here · synced".
- **Pane buttons have their shape in every theme**, with the theme's own
  colors.
- A few messages are worded in sentence case, for example "Switch to source
  mode to edit it".

## Fixed

- The repair dialog showed a raw marker line, `<!-- lfcp-ref … -->`,
  instead of the task's text in its choices, and the pane could show
  **"Repair shared sections…"** twice and two status lines saying the same
  thing ([#87](https://github.com/openlfcp/obsidian/issues/87)).
- Timers of shared sections kept running for a moment after the plugin was
  turned off ([#81](https://github.com/openlfcp/obsidian/issues/81)).

## Install and upgrade

From Settings → Community plugins → Updates. Nothing to migrate: identity,
keys, shared sections, queued edits, settings and hotkeys are kept.

By hand: put `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from the release
page into `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/shared-tasks/` and compare them with
these checksums.

| File | SHA-256 |
| --- | --- |

Built by CI from the commit the tag `0.6.0` points to; the release page
carries a build provenance attestation: `gh attestation verify main.js
--repo openlfcp/obsidian`.

## Rolling back to 0.5.2

Install 0.5.2 over 0.6.0 (BRAT: choose version 0.5.2 for
`openlfcp/obsidian`, or put 0.5.2's three files into the plugin folder).
The data format is the same; the pane looks as it did in 0.5.2.

## Known limits

As in [0.5.1](0.5.1.md#known-limits), plus:

- With one note open in two panes, cutting a task in one pane and pasting
  it into the other right away can lose the task, mostly on Windows: Obsidian
  saves the first pane while the second merges the change, and the merge
  drops the moved task; the deletion then syncs. Paste it again from the
  clipboard, or move tasks within one pane.
