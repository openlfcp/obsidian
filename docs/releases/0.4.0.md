Shared Tasks 0.4.0 (beta): share a whole section of a note.

> **DRAFT** (LFCP-02-093), not released. `<TBD>` marks what the release
> candidate still settles.

**Requires Obsidian 1.13.4 or later**, as 0.3.2 did.

## New: shared sections

- **"Share section…".** Put the cursor on a heading, or under it, and share
  the heading with everything under it: tasks, subtasks, paragraphs and
  lists, in order, down to the next heading of the same or a higher level.
  - A preview shows exactly what will be shared, and what under the
    heading stays private on your device.
  - Each section is a collaboration of its own, with its own key and its
    own invitation.
  - Whatever is added inside the section later is shared too.
- **Joining.** Your collaborator runs "Join collaboration" with the
  one-time link, then "Insert shared section…" in any note of theirs. Their
  other text stays private.
- **Status marks.** A double tick (✓✓) after a shared heading means
  "shared". A separate icon shows sync progress, for example "Saved
  locally; waiting to sync", "Offline; local updates will wait" or "Needs
  your attention". Click it, or run "Open shared section details", to see
  the details: the status, what is shared, and who has access, as last
  verified with the server.
- **Conflicts.** A task moved to two places, or a field changed on both
  sides, shows as "Needs your attention". "Go to next shared section
  problem" finds it; "Review conflicts…" in the details lets you choose.
  Nothing is lost while a choice is open.
- **Removing access.** "Remove access…" in the details removes someone's
  future access and gives the section a new key. Copies they already
  received cannot be erased, and the confirmation says so. On their side,
  the section says their access was removed; their new edits stay on their
  device.
- **Detach this section.** Stops sharing a section in one note and keeps its
  text as your own. The shared section, your other notes and your
  collaborators are unchanged.
- **Copying.** "Copy readable text (without sharing metadata)" copies the
  text without the section's markers. "Copy shared section" copies it with
  them: pasted into another note, it is another copy of the same section,
  and it gives nobody access.
- **Repair.** "Repair shared sections in this note" puts a damaged boundary
  back, points to lines it cannot match, and lets you choose between your
  version and the shared one.
- **Moving 0.3 shared tasks into a section** is an explicit copy offered by
  "Share section…". "Restore note before section import" undoes it in the
  note.

In the note, a section carries one comment after its heading, one at its
end and one beside each item. Live Preview hides them; Source mode, or
"Show or hide sharing metadata", shows them.

## Also new

- **"Export diagnostics…"** prepares a report of versions, states and error
  codes, and shows it to you in full first. It holds no note text, names,
  paths, keys or invitation links. You copy it or save it as a file;
  nothing is sent.
- **Local sync data is encrypted at rest** on each device, under a key kept
  on that device. "Rotate key" (Settings → Shared Tasks → "Local
  encryption") replaces it.
- **New members catch up faster.** The owner's plugin publishes Snapshots,
  so a new member loads one plus the latest changes, not the whole history.
- **After a server restore,** a member the server had forgotten gets back in
  by themselves.

## Limits

- **Size.** A section of about 200 tasks with their notes is the qualified
  size. A much larger one (2,000 tasks) works, but each edit blocks the
  editor for seconds.
- **Long history.** After many thousands of edits, a section becomes slower
  (about half a second per edit). `<TBD: the history growth policy,
  LFCP-02-112>`
- **Mobile.** Sections are read-only, or marked unsupported, on mobile.
  Shared tasks work there as before. `<TBD: as implemented, LFCP-02-095>`
- **No headings inside a section.** Sharing offers to split it into
  sections.
- **Local tokens.** Some Tasks-plugin tokens stay local to your note (🔁 🛫
  ➕ ❌ 🔼). The preview shows them.

## Updating from 0.3

> **Updating to 0.4.** Your shared tasks keep working as before. Shared
> Tasks upgrades its sync data on this device the first time 0.4 starts: it
> is encrypted at rest, and earlier versions can no longer open it.
>
> **Going back.** After updating to 0.4, Shared Tasks cannot go back to
> 0.3.x on this device: its local sync data is upgraded and encrypted.
> Downgrade only to 0.4.x or later. Your notes are not affected. If a 0.3.x
> plugin shows "Shared Tasks could not start: VersionError", that is why:
> install 0.4.x again, and its data is intact.

A collaborator still on 0.3.x cannot join a shared section: their plugin
says a newer version is needed, without using up the link.

## Install and upgrade

From Community plugins (Settings → Community plugins → Updates), with BRAT
(`openlfcp/obsidian`), or by hand: put `main.js`, `manifest.json` and
`styles.css` from the release page into
`<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/shared-tasks/`. Your identity, collaborations and
settings are kept. How to use sections:
[shared sections guide](../guides/shared-sections.md).
