# Shared Tasks 0.7.2

Shared Tasks 0.7.2 explains when this device cannot open its local data,
instead of leaving shared sections loading forever. For catalog users
updating from 0.7.1.

**Requires Obsidian 1.13.4 or later**, as 0.7.1.

The Markdown format of your notes and the sync protocol are unchanged.

## What changed since 0.7.1

- **A locked device says why.** The pane, section list, details card,
  badges and status bar explain when Shared Tasks cannot open this
  device's local data. The pane offers **Open settings**. Device commands
  explain the same problem instead of asking you to try again later.
- **Recovery gives both steps.** On a locked device, choose **Create a
  new identity** in Settings → Shared Tasks, then **Pair this device with
  a link…**. Get the link with **Add my other device…** on your first
  device or another with Full access. Creating the new identity restarts
  Shared Tasks without restarting Obsidian. Nothing is reset automatically.
  A new identity alone is a separate person without your earlier sections.
  See [Your devices](../guides/devices.md#2-add-a-device) for pairing and
  what to do if no other device can approve it.
- **Details open before loading finishes.** The card says which facts are
  still unknown, including pending changes and access. It updates when
  startup finds a lock and gains its available actions when the section
  loads. If another window holds the data, it advises closing that window
  or restarting Obsidian; it does not offer an identity reset.
- **Diagnostics describe the actual state.** A locked runtime includes its
  reason and is no longer logged as ready. An iPhone or iPad is identified
  as iOS rather than macOS.

## Install and upgrade

From Settings → Community plugins → Updates. Nothing to migrate.

By hand: put `main.js`, `manifest.json` and `styles.css` from the release
page into `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/shared-tasks/` and compare them with
these checksums.

## Rolling back to 0.7.1

Install 0.7.1 over 0.7.2 (BRAT: choose version 0.7.1 for
`openlfcp/obsidian`, or put 0.7.1's three files into the plugin folder). The
data format is the same; locked devices may show endless loading again.
Rolling back the plugin does not undo an identity you explicitly created.

## Known limits

The limits in [0.7.1](0.7.1.md#known-limits) still apply. In particular,
shared sections on phones remain read-only.

- The locked-device recovery and pairing path was tested with simulated
  devices against the reference server, but not on a real iPhone or iPad
  with an iCloud-synced vault.
- Pairing needs your first device or another with Full access to approve
  it. There is no recovery kit in this version. Without such a device,
  collaborators must invite the new identity again.
- A locked device may still hold unsent changes; the card cannot inspect
  them. Pairing a new identity does not recover the old identity's local
  sync queue. Your note files are not reset by creating a new identity.
